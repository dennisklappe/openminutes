# Consent and legal notes

You decide when and where OpenMinutes records and you must follow the laws and policies that apply. This is general information, not legal advice.

## What OpenMinutes does

- The bot joins under a visible name, never hidden.
- It can post a join message in the meeting chat (the default mentions OpenMinutes).
- Meeting status and the participant list show a bot is present.
- Everything stays on your server, and you control how long audio is kept.

## What you should do

- **Tell people before recording**, in the invite or at the start. Often a legal requirement.
- **Know the local rule.** Some places need one party's consent, some all parties'; across borders the strictest can apply.
- **Check data protection law.** Recordings and transcripts are personal data. In the EU and UK you need a lawful basis, a retention limit and a way to honour access and deletion requests. Set retention in Settings, Storage and retention.
- **Check workplace policy.** Employers, clients and some industries restrict recording regardless.
- **Choose providers on purpose.** Hosted transcription or summary providers receive the audio or text. Use local transcription for sensitive meetings.
- **Respect a no.** Remove the bot and delete the recording.

## Ending a recording

Remove the bot like any participant. It stops and keeps what it recorded. Delete the meeting from its detail page to remove audio, transcript and summary.
